AgentEnglish

Write in Chinese to translate, or write in English to polish your grammar and phrasing. Pause to see the suggestion. The panel grows to fit the suggestion. Choose Copy for the entire finished result, or the speaker to hear it aloud.

Terminal

Open a new zsh tab in Ghostty, Terminal or iTerm2. Start claude or codex. Restart any sessions opened before installation.

Desktop

Choose AgentEnglish → Enable Desktop Access and grant Accessibility permission in System Settings. Return to the message field in Claude, ChatGPT or Codex. AgentEnglish also recognizes the Herder Mac app’s native prompt editor; its current source targets macOS 26. Herder terminal sessions are not verified.

Translation and speech

Use Work for technical English or Everyday for conversation. Both polish English and translate Chinese using the local Ollama model qwen3:4b-instruct. Apple Offline uses the English and Traditional Chinese languages downloaded on your Mac. Choose Slow Speech for slower playback.
